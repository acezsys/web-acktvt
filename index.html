Itemised notices, security safeguards, breach intimation, retention, rights timelines and children's-data rules all become enforceable at once.

Rules 3, 6–8, 10, 13 · Sections 5–14
THE CLOCKS72 hours. 90 days.

Breach: affected people told without delay, full report to the Board within 72 hours. Rights requests and grievances: answered within 90 days — after that, the person may go to the Board.

Rule 7 · Sections 11–14 · Rule 14
UP TO ₹250 CRPenalty per instance

The maximum for failing reasonable security safeguards. The Board works as a digital office — complaints arrive online, and "did you know?" needs a written answer.
